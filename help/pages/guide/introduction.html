Introduction

LOGIK-PROJEKT is an advanced toolkit designed for the creation, business continuity, and lifecycle management of Digital Content Creation (DCC) projects.

Purpose

The primary goal of LOGIK-PROJEKT is to automate and standardize project setups. This allows creative stakeholders—artists, producers, and developers—to focus on their creative tasks rather than being bogged down by technical impediments or manual filesystem organization.

Focus: While versatile, LOGIK-PROJEKT was developed with a heavy emphasis on Autodesk Flame workflows.

Key Capabilities

  • Automated Directory Structures: Generate consistent, configurable folder hierarchies for any project.
  • Flame Integration: Direct creation of Autodesk Flame projects with custom setups and media directories.
  • Dynamic Bookmarks: Automatically link filesystem folders to Flame project reels and library folders.
  • Knowledge Management: Integrated RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) and MCP (Model Context Protocol) support for AI-assisted development.

Workflow Concept

LOGIK-PROJEKT operates on the principle of a "Portal Root Directory" (e.g., /PROJEKTS). By standardizing the entry point for all project data, the toolkit ensures that projects are easily portable across workstations and servers, facilitating robust backups and disaster recovery.
